    How it works
    • The dealer always turns up a number card. Non-numbers are burned.
    • Play a card that matches the dealer’s colour or number, or any wild. An action card can therefore only ever match by colour.
    • Every card scores 1, a Skip scores 2 — but never past the jackpot.
    • A Reverse lets you immediately play a rider alongside it: same colour as the Reverse, any wild, or any Reverse. A rider that is itself a Reverse grants another, matched against the card you just played.
    • +2 draws 2 cards and +4 draws 4, after the turn resolves. A plain Wild draws nothing. Those draws are how a five-card hand climbs the table.
    • The round ends when nothing in hand matches, the hand empties, or you hit the jackpot. You are paid the tier you finish on.
